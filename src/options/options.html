What each check reads

One decision path, two context paths

  1. 01
    Learned visual fingerprints

    The frozen FP32 ViT candidate runs over a 384×384 representation in WebAssembly and alone determines the calibration-gate score.

  2. 02
    Generator traces

    PNG, JPEG, WebP, and AVIF bytes are checked for prompts, workflow data, and generator names, then reported as context only.

  3. 03
    Provenance & watermark clues

    C2PA containers, Content Credentials, SynthID labels, and known embedded watermark labels are surfaced without altering the model decision or claiming the invisible mark was decoded.
